A real hospital, in Maple Hospital Launching soon

Maple Hospital check-in with real patient charts. When you arrive, you check in, staff see you on a live board, a nurse calls you to a room, everything they do for you is written in your own patient record, and you leave with discharge papers you can keep.

How a visit works

  1. ArriveCome into the hospital in our Maple Hospital custom server.
  2. Check inAt reception in game, or online before you arrive. Tell us what's wrong.
  3. Called to a roomStaff see you on a live board. A nurse calls you and takes you to your room.
  4. TreatedChecks, tests and treatment. Everything is written in your patient record.
  5. Go homeLeave with discharge papers you can keep, and rate your care.

Our departments

Six teams run the hospital, each with its own ranks, training and leaders.

Nursing

The heart of the ward: assessing patients, vitals and care from arrival to discharge.

Medical

Doctors who review patients, order tests and decide on a diagnosis.

Surgical

Surgeons who take patients from Pre-Op through to theatre and recovery.

Paramedicine

Paramedics who answer emergency calls and bring patients into the hospital.

Administration

The team that keeps the hospital running: sessions, moderation and support.

Community Engagement

Events, news and a warm welcome for new members.
